# YUMRAM v5.2.41 — Automatic Post-Scan Research + Persistent Knowledge

- Intelligence Scan remains manually initiated.
- After a scan, unresolved live records are automatically queued for research when `AutoResearchAfterScan` is enabled (default: true).
- `Run Research` remains available for manual retry/research after the automatic pass completes or when errors remain.
- Research results continue to persist in the Intelligence database and research cache for future sessions.
- Scan UI messaging now distinguishes automatic research from manual retry.
