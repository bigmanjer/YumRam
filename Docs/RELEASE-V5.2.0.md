# YUMRAM V5.2.2 — Automatic Review Resolution

## Review queue behavior

Review is a temporary research state. Every non-manual Review item is automatically processed through local evidence and, when still unresolved, online corroboration.

Resolved items are automatically assigned to a concrete placement. Items that remain unresolved after the available research is exhausted are moved to `Unknown / Quarantine for Review`.

Manual organization overrides automatic research and is not overwritten by the automatic resolver.

## UI status

The Intelligence view reports total researched records, Review items resolved into categories, and unresolved items moved to Unknown.
